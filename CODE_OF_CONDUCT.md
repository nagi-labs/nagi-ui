# Code of Conduct

Nagi UI contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
inclusive, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing another person's
private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
